BALOCHI LANGUAGE PLATFORM

About Balochi Language

A long-term digital initiative to document, teach and develop technology for Balochi.

Our vision

We aim to create a reliable, searchable and community-focused digital home for Balochi, beginning with dictionary and learning resources and growing toward translation, speech resources and AI-assisted language technology.

Authentic data

Balochi has multiple regional varieties, so the platform records dialect information instead of treating every form as identical.

Project principle

Preserve the language. Respect its dialects. Verify the data. Build the technology responsibly.
